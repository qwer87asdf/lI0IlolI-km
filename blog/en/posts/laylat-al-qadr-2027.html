Laylat al-Qadr 2027: Expected Dates, Dua and How to Prepare

Hasan · 5 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

Laylat al-Qadr 2027 is expected to fall on one of the odd nights in the last ten nights of Ramadan 1448. Ramadan is expected to begin on about 8 February 2027, which puts the first of those ten nights on the evening of about 27 February. Nobody knows the exact night, and that is part of the gift: it invites you to give the whole stretch your attention.

Here are the odd nights with their expected evenings. Remember that an Islamic night begins at sunset, so each night starts the evening before its day.

  • 21st night: expected evening of about 27 February 2027
  • 23rd night: expected evening of about 1 March 2027
  • 25th night: expected evening of about 3 March 2027
  • 27th night: expected evening of about 5 March 2027
  • 29th night: expected evening of about 7 March 2027

All of these dates are expected, not confirmed. Ramadan starts with local moon sighting or your local authority’s announcement, so your night may shift by a day.

What is Laylat al-Qadr?

Laylat al-Qadr is often translated as the Night of Decree or the Night of Power. It is the night on which the Qur’an began to be revealed, and the Qur’an gives it a weight no other night has.

Surah Al-Qadr (chapter 97)tells us that the Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. A thousand months is more than 83 years, so one night of sincere worship can outweigh a full lifetime of ordinary nights.

Surah Ad-Dukhan (44:3)also refers to the night, describing it as a blessed night on which the Qur’an was sent down.

For the reader, the point is practical. You do not need special knowledge or a perfect record to use this night. You need to show up with sincerity and make the most of the time you have.

Which night is Laylat al-Qadr?

The Prophet (peace be upon him) told the believers tolook for Laylat al-Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan.

Many Muslims lean towards the 27th night, and this view has a long history in the Muslim community. It is not the only view. Some scholars have favoured other odd nights, and some have said it can move from year to year. So the 27th night is a strong candidate in the minds of many, and still not a certainty.

Why would the night be left unspecified? Scholars have offered reasons. One often-cited reflection is that a hidden night encourages people to worship across all of the last ten nights instead of one.

If you are asking about the 27th night of Ramadan 2027, the expected evening is about 5 March. If you can only give a few nights real effort, the odd nights are the ones the hadith points to. Giving all ten is better still.

The Laylat al-Qadr dua

The dua most associated with this night comes froma narration of Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her). She asked the Prophet (peace be upon him) what she should say if she found Laylat al-Qadr, and he taught her a short dua for forgiveness.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

(Sunan Ibn Majah 3850;Jami’ at-Tirmidhi 3513)

Some printings of Tirmidhi add the wordkarim; the shorter wording above is the one most scholars consider established.

Transliteration: Allahumma innaka ‘afuwwun tuhibbul ‘afwa fa’fu ‘anni

Meaning: O Allah, You are the Pardoner, You love to pardon, so pardon me.

It is short enough to memorise in a few minutes and to repeat in sujood, after prayer or while you walk around the house. You can also add your own du’a in your own language. Many people write a short list beforehand so the important requests do not slip away at 3 a.m.

A simple plan for the last ten nights

You do not need an elaborate schedule. A plan you can keep for ten nights beats a perfect one that lasts two.

Qiyam

Pray extra units at night after Isha, or closer to Fajr if that is when you can stay awake. Even a few rak’ahs counts. If you pray taraweeh, consider staying with the imam until the end.

Qur’an

Set a small, realistic amount and keep to it. A page after each prayer adds up. If you can, include Surah Al-Qadr in your recitation and take a minute to read its meaning.

A du’a list

Write down what you want to ask for: forgiveness, family, health, guidance, people you love who are struggling. Keep the list by your prayer mat. Include the dua above.

Charity

Give something on each night, even if it is small. Setting up a standing donation for the ten nights takes the decision out of a tired evening.

I’tikaf

I’tikaf means staying in the mosque for worship, and many Muslims do it in the last ten days. It has its own conditions, so this article does not cover them. See our separate guide on i’tikaf.

If you work or have young children

The last ten nights do not ask you to be someone else. If you work during the day, choose one fixed slot, such as 30 minutes after Isha, and protect it. If you have young children, split the night with your spouse, or pray your qiyam at home in short turns. Short, regular worship done sincerely is real worship.

Signs of Laylat al-Qadr mentioned in hadith

People share many signs of Laylat al-Qadr online, and a lot of them have no source. This section sticks to what is reported in the hadith.

One narration describes thatthe sun rises the following morning without strong rays.

That is the only sign included here, because it is the one our research notes support.

You do not need to see a sign to benefit from the night. Most people will not notice anything unusual. Worship the whole last ten nights, and leave the rest to Allah.

Laylat al-Qadr 2027 FAQ

When is Laylat al-Qadr 2027?

It is expected to fall on one of the odd nights in the last ten nights of Ramadan 2027, with the first of those nights expected on the evening of about 27 February. The exact night is not known.

Is Laylat al-Qadr always on the 27th?

No ruling says so. Many Muslims favour the 27th night, but scholars have also pointed to other odd nights.

What dua should I read on Laylat al-Qadr?

Allahumma innaka ‘afuwwun tuhibbul ‘afwa fa’fu ‘anni, taught by the Prophet (peace be upon him) to Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her).

What are the signs of Laylat al-Qadr?

One reported sign is that the sun rises the next morning without strong rays. Signs are not a condition for receiving the reward.

Can women on their period benefit from Laylat al-Qadr?

The mainstream view is that a woman on her period cannot pray or fast, but she can still make du’a and dhikr, and she can seek the night through them. Scholars differ on details such as reciting the Qur’an.

Get ready for Laylat al-Qadr 2027

Laylat al-Qadr 2027 is months away, and that is a good thing. You have time to choose your nights, write your du’a list and decide what you can keep up. Pick the dua above, learn it, and keep it ready for the evening of about 27 February.

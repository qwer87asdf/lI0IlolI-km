10 Must-Have Muslim Lifestyle Apps for New Muslims

Muslims Day Desk · 30 Jul 2026 · 10 min read

Embracing Islam is a beautiful, life-changing journey – but for new Muslims (reverts), it can also feel overwhelming at first. Learning the prayers, memorizing duas, understanding the Quran, and building consistent habits all take time. Thankfully, technology has made this transition significantly easier. A good Muslim lifestyle app can act as a daily companion, guiding you through prayer times, Quran recitation, duas, and more – all from your phone.

In this listicle, we’re breaking down the must-have Muslim lifestyle apps every new Muslim should consider downloading, starting with the most beginner-friendly, all-in-one option.

  1. Muslims Day – Best all-in-one app for beginners (prayer times, Quran, duas, Qibla, Bangla + English support)
  2. Muslim Pro – Most popular global Muslim lifestyle app with Islamic content, community feed, and habit tracking

  7. Tarteel – AI-powered app for learning Quran recitation and memorization
  8. Zabihah -The go-to halal restaurant, market, and prayer-space finder
  9. Sunnah.com App – Authentic hadith collections for building Islamic knowledge
  10. Muslim & Quran – Prayer time app with prayer history logging and multilingual support

Read on for a detailed breakdown of each.

1. Muslims Day – Best All-in-One App for New Muslims

If you’re just starting your journey and want one app that covers everything without complexity, Muslims Day is the top pick. It’s designed with simplicity in mind, making it especially beginner-friendly for new Muslims who are still learning the basics of daily worship.

Key Features:

  • 🕌 Accurate prayer times for all five daily prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), plus optional prayers like Duha, Tahajjud, and Ishrak
  • ⏱️ Prayer alarms, notifications, and a countdown timer for the current Salat window
  • 📖 Quran, Tafseer, and Hadith access
  • 📿 Daily duas and Asmaul Husna (99 Names of Allah)
  • 🧭 Built-in Qibla compass
  • 🌍 Available in both English and Bangla – ideal for Bengali-speaking new Muslims and their families
  • 🌙 Special seasonal content, such as Ramadan Sehri/Iftar timings and virtues of Dhul-Hijjah

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: As a new Muslim, having a single, uncluttered app that walks you through your five daily prayers – with reminders and simple explanations – removes a lot of the early guesswork. Muslims Day keeps everything essential in one place, without overwhelming you with unnecessary features.

2. Muslim Pro – Most Popular Global Muslim Lifestyle App

Muslim Pro is one of the most widely downloaded Muslim lifestyle apps in the world, with a large global user base. It offers a comprehensive suite of features, making it a strong choice for new Muslims who want a feature-rich experience.

Key Features:

  • Accurate, location-based prayer times with customizable Athan (Adhan) alerts
  • Full Holy Quran with translations in 40+ languages, audio recitations, and memorization tools
  • Qibla compass and Islamic (Hijri) calendar
  • Daily duas and azkar, plus a digital Tasbih counter
  • Halal restaurant and mosque finder
  • Community feed to connect with other Muslims
  • Islamic educational content, including films and articles (via Qalbox)

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: The sheer breadth of content – from beginner-friendly Quran tools to a built-in community feed – makes Muslim Pro a solid choice for reverts who want to explore Islamic learning materials alongside their daily prayer routine. Note that some advanced features require a premium subscription.

3. IslamicFinder – Trusted Source for Prayer Times & Islamic Knowledge

IslamicFinder has long been recognized as one of the most trusted names for accurate prayer times and Islamic educational resources, both on the web and through its mobile apps.

Key Features:

  • Verified, location-based prayer times using multiple calculation methods
  • Qibla direction finder
  • Islamic calendar with important dates (Ramadan, Eid, etc.)
  • Access to Quran, Hadith, and dua collections
  • Zakat calculator and Islamic date converter
  • Regularly updated blog content covering Islamic knowledge and lifestyle topics

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: Beyond just prayer times, IslamicFinder’s strength lies in its educational content – making it a good companion app for new Muslims who want to deepen their understanding of Islamic practices alongside their daily worship routine.

5. The Pillars – Minimalist, Privacy-Focused Prayer App

For new Muslims who want a simple, distraction-free way to build the habit of praying five times a day, The Pillars is an excellent choice. Built by Muslims with a strong focus on privacy, this app strips away unnecessary clutter and focuses on the essentials.

Key Features:

  • Clean, minimalist prayer times and Qibla compass
  • Prayer tracker – specifically designed to help new converts and those building consistency in their salah
  • Fasting tracker to log missed fasts and mark makeup days
  • A “menstruation pause” feature so tracking streaks aren’t broken
  • Home screen and lock screen widgets for quick access
  • Strong privacy focus – location data stays on your device and isn’t sent to external servers

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: The built-in prayer tracker is particularly valuable for reverts, since it’s specifically designed to help you build the habit of praying consistently – one of the most important early steps in a new Muslim’s journey.

5. Athan – Reliable, No-Frills Prayer & Qibla App

Athan, developed by IslamicFinder, is a straightforward, no-frills app focused specifically on two core needs: prayer time notifications and Qibla direction.

Key Features:

  • Automatic Athan (call to prayer) playback at prayer times
  • Built-in compass for accurate Qibla direction
  • 12-hour and 24-hour time format options
  • Quran recitations
  • Simple, easy-to-navigate interface

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: If you’re looking for an app that does the basics extremely well without extra clutter, Athan is a dependable option. Its simplicity makes it especially approachable for someone who’s still getting used to the rhythm of daily prayers.

6. Muslim Bangla – Best for Bengali-Speaking New Muslims

For Bengali-speaking new Muslims, or those with family and community ties to Bangladesh, Muslim Bangla offers a comprehensive Islamic lifestyle experience fully localized in the Bangla language.

Key Features:

  • Prayer times and Adhan alerts in Bangla and English
  • Full Quran with Bangla translation and recitation
  • Hadith collections (Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, and more)
  • Daily dua and Zakat/Sadaka tools
  • “Ask Scholars” Q&A feature and voice call access to a Mufti for direct guidance
  • Qibla compass, mosque finder, and Hajj/Umrah guide
  • Islamic calendar, 99 Names of Allah, and live Makkah streaming

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: Language matters a lot in the early stages of learning Islam. For Bengali speakers, having prayer instructions, Quran translations, and scholarly guidance available in their native language makes the learning curve significantly smoother.

7. Tarteel – AI-Powered Quran Recitation & Memorization

For new Muslims eager to learn to read and eventually memorize the Quran, Tarteel is one of the most innovative tools available. It uses AI-powered voice recognition to listen to your recitation and give real-time feedback.

Key Features:

  • Voice-recognition technology that listens as you recite and highlights mistakes in real time
  • “Shazam for Quran” – recite any phrase and the app instantly finds the matching Ayah and Surah
  • Mistake history tracking to help you focus on recurring errors
  • Multiple Qira’at (recitation styles) and reciter options
  • Hidden verse/peeking mode to test memorization progress
  • Translations and tafsir available alongside the Arabic text

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: Learning to read Arabic script and recite the Quran correctly can feel intimidating at first. Tarteel acts like a patient, always-available practice partner – useful for building confidence before eventually learning from a qualified teacher.

8. Zabihah – Find Halal Food & Prayer Spaces Anywhere

Figuring out what’s halal to eat – and where to find it – is one of the most common early challenges for new Muslims. Zabihah has been the go-to halal restaurant and market finder for decades, with one of the largest global databases in the space.

Key Features:

  • Search over 50,000 halal restaurants, markets, and butchers worldwide
  • Detailed listings with halal certification info, hours, pricing, and reviews
  • Nearby mosque and prayer space finder – especially useful when traveling
  • Community reviews and social feed for recommendations
  • Delivery links through major platforms where available

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: Adjusting to halal dietary guidelines is a major part of the transition into Islamic practice. Zabihah takes the guesswork out of finding trustworthy halal options nearby or while traveling.

9. Sunnah.com App – Authentic Hadith at Your Fingertips

Understanding the sayings and practices of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is a key part of growing in Islamic knowledge. The Sunnah.com App, built on the widely trusted Sunnah.com database, gives you direct access to major hadith collections.

Key Features:

  • Full collections including Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, and Sunan Abi Dawud, among others
  • Arabic and English translations side by side
  • Powerful search across collections by keyword or topic
  • Save favorite hadiths and share them easily
  • Daily hadith feature and author biographies for context

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: Beyond the Quran, hadith literature is essential for understanding how to practice Islam day to day. Having a searchable, authenticated hadith library on your phone makes it easy to look up guidance on specific topics as questions come up.

10. Muslim & Quran – Prayer Logging Made Simple

Rounding out the list is Muslim & Quran, a prayer times app with a particular focus on helping users log and review their prayer history over time.

Key Features:

  • Accurate, location-based prayer times with daylight saving time adjustment
  • PrayerBook feature to log completed prayers and view your history
  • Multiple calculation methods and Fiqh/madhab preferences
  • Customizable notification sounds for prayer alerts
  • Printable Islamic calendar and Ramadan calendar

Why It’s Great for New Muslims: Being able to look back and see your prayer consistency over weeks or months can be genuinely motivating in the early stages of building a prayer habit – turning an invisible personal effort into something you can track and reflect on.

How to Choose the Right App for You

With several solid options available, here’s a simple way to decide:

  • Want everything simplified in one place? Start with Muslims Day.
  • Want the most content-rich, globally popular option? Try Muslim Pro.
  • Want a trusted, educational-focused resource? Go with IslamicFinder.
  • Want to focus purely on building consistent prayer habits, privately? Choose The Pillars.
  • Want something simple that just handles Athan and Qibla? Go with Athan.
  • Are you a Bengali speaker? Muslim Bangla will feel the most natural and accessible.
  • Want to learn to recite and memorize the Quran? Tarteel is built exactly for that.
  • Need to find halal food or a place to pray while out? Zabihah has you covered.
  • Want to dive deeper into hadith and Islamic knowledge? Check out the Sunnah.com App.
  • Want to track your prayer history over time? Muslim & Quran makes that easy.

Many new Muslims actually use two or three apps together – for example, one focused on prayer tracking and habit-building, and another for deeper Quran study or community connection. There’s no single “correct” combination – it comes down to what helps you stay consistent.

Final Thoughts

Becoming a Muslim is a journey of learning, patience, and consistency – and the right tools can make that journey significantly smoother. Whether you choose an all-in-one Muslim lifestyle app like Muslims Day, a feature-rich option like Muslim Pro, or a language-specific app like Muslim Bangla, what matters most is finding one (or a combination) that helps you stay consistent in your daily worship.

Start simple, be patient with yourself, and let these apps support – not replace – the genuine effort of building a personal connection with your faith, one prayer at a time.

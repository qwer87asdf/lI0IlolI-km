When Does Ramadan 2027 Start?

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin in February, bringing the holy month of fasting, prayer, Quran recitation, charity, and spiritual reflection to Muslims around the world.

According to commonly used Islamic calendars, the first day of Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to fall on Monday, February 8, 2027. However, communities that rely on local crescent-moon sightings may begin fasting on Tuesday, February 9 instead.

The exact date depends on the method used to determine the beginning of the Islamic month and the official announcement made by the relevant religious authority.

Why Does Ramadan Start on Different Dates in Different Countries?

The Islamic calendar is based on the lunar cycle rather than the solar year used by the Gregorian calendar.

Each Islamic month begins with the new lunar month, which is traditionally established through crescent-moon sighting. Because the moon’s visibility depends on several factors, determining the beginning of Ramadan can involve both astronomical information and religious guidance.

There are three important reasons why dates may differ.

1. Local Moon Sighting

Some countries and communities require the crescent moon to be sighted locally or within a designated geographical region.

If the crescent cannot be confirmed on the expected evening, the preceding Islamic month may be completed as 30 days. Ramadan then begins the following day.

This approach follows the traditional practice of establishing the new month through crescent-moon observation.

2. Regional or Global Moon Sighting

Other communities accept a confirmed sighting from a wider geographical area or follow an established regional authority.

For example, one community may accept a verified sighting from another country, while another may require evidence from within its own region.

Consequently, two communities can make different decisions even when they have access to similar astronomical information.

3. Astronomical Calculations

Some Islamic organizations use astronomical calculations to determine dates in advance.

These calculations can estimate when the new moon occurs, when the moon sets relative to the sun, and whether the young crescent is likely to be visible.

However, the astronomical new moon and the first visible crescent are not the same thing. A new moon may occur before the crescent becomes visible after sunset.

Different calendar authorities also apply different criteria when deciding whether a month has begun. This is one reason calculated dates can differ from dates established through physical sighting.

How Does Moon Sighting Work?

Moon sighting is one of the most important traditions associated with the beginning and end of Ramadan.

After the 29th day of Sha’ban, the month before Ramadan, observers look for the thin crescent shortly after sunset.

The process generally involves the following steps:

  1. The expected observation evening arrives. Astronomers and moon-sighting committees identify when and where the crescent may be visible.
  2. Observers look toward the western horizon. The young crescent is usually sought shortly after sunset, weather and visibility permitting.
  3. Reports are collected. Depending on local procedures, witnesses may submit their observations to an authorized committee.
  4. The relevant authority evaluates the reports. The authority applies its established religious and astronomical criteria.
  5. The official announcement is made. Muslims are informed whether the next day will be the first of Ramadan or whether Sha’ban will be completed as 30 days.

Cloud cover, atmospheric conditions, the moon’s position, and the interval between sunset and moonset can affect visibility.

Modern technology can help astronomers predict visibility, but different religious authorities may still follow different procedures for accepting sightings.

For this reason, Ramadan dates published months in advance should always be treated as estimates when the final date depends on an official sighting.

When Is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr, the festival marking the end of Ramadan, is expected to fall on Tuesday, March 9, or Wednesday, March 10, 2027.

The exact date depends on the beginning of Ramadan and the sighting of the crescent of Shawwal, the Islamic month that follows Ramadan.

If Ramadan begins on February 8 and lasts 29 days, Eid is expected on March 9. If the relevant calendar or moon-sighting decision places Eid one day later, it may fall on March 10.

Eid al-Fitr begins the Islamic month of Shawwal and is celebrated with the special Eid prayer, family gatherings, charity, and festive meals.

Muslims also give Zakat al-Fitr, a prescribed charity intended to help eligible people in need participate in the celebration. Its amount, timing, and practical requirements should be checked with a trusted local Islamic authority.

Because Eid dates are tied to the lunar calendar, anyone arranging travel, school holidays, family gatherings, or workplace leave should keep some flexibility in their plans.

How Long Will Ramadan 2027 Last?

Ramadan lasts either 29 or 30 days.

The length depends on the establishment of the next Islamic month, Shawwal. If the Shawwal crescent is confirmed according to the applicable rules after the 29th day of Ramadan, the month ends after 29 days. Otherwise, Ramadan completes 30 days.

For Ramadan 2027, the expected final fasting day is around March 8 or March 9, with Eid al-Fitr expected around March 9 or March 10.

A calendar published in advance may show a 29-day month, but communities that rely on moon sighting must wait for the relevant announcement before confirming the end date.

How to Prepare for Ramadan 2027

Although Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin in February, preparing early can help you approach the month with greater focus and consistency.

Here are five practical steps.

1. Prepare Your Worship Routine

Set realistic goals for Salah, Quran recitation, dua, and other acts of worship. Choose a routine that fits your responsibilities and that you can maintain throughout the month.

2. Adjust Your Sleep Schedule

Gradually prepare for waking up before dawn for suhoor and Fajr. Planning your sleep in advance can make the transition easier.

3. Plan Your Meals

Prepare balanced meal options for suhoor and iftar. Include nutritious foods, drink water regularly between sunset and dawn, and avoid making every iftar an excessively heavy meal.

4. Organize Your Responsibilities

If possible, complete important tasks before Ramadan and make a flexible schedule for work, studies, family commitments, and worship.

5. Set Spiritual Goals

Think about what you want to improve during Ramadan. You might focus on understanding the Quran, becoming more consistent with prayer, giving charity, or improving your character.

The purpose is not to create an impossible checklist. It is to make meaningful progress through sincere and consistent effort.

Frequently Asked Questions About Ramadan 2027

1. What is the exact date of Ramadan 2027?

The first fast is expected on Monday, February 8, 2027, in many calculated calendars. Some communities, including those that depend on local moon sighting, may begin on Tuesday, February 9. The final date will depend on the relevant announcement.

2. When is the first Taraweeh prayer in 2027?

If the first fast is Monday, February 8, the first Taraweeh prayers are expected after sunset on Sunday, February 7. If the first fast is Tuesday, February 9, Taraweeh would generally begin after sunset on Monday, February 8.

3. When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr is expected on Tuesday, March 9, or Wednesday, March 10, 2027. The confirmed date depends on the calendar or moon-sighting method followed by your community.

4. Will Ramadan 2027 begin on the same day worldwide?

Not necessarily. Differences in geographical moon visibility, local and regional sighting policies, and calculated calendars can lead to a one-day difference between communities.

5. Why can Ramadan dates change at the last minute?

A predicted date may change because the crescent is not confirmed according to the relevant authority’s criteria. If the preceding Islamic month reaches 30 days, Ramadan begins the following day.

6. Will Ramadan 2027 last 29 or 30 days?

It may last either 29 or 30 days. The end of Ramadan is established according to the beginning of Shawwal, using the relevant moon-sighting or calendar method.

7. Is Ramadan 2027 in February or March?

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin in February and continue into March. Eid al-Fitr is expected in March.

8. How can I find Ramadan 2027 Sehri and Iftar times?

Sehri and iftar times depend on your city and local prayer-time calculation method. Check a reliable local timetable or a trusted Islamic app, and verify the times against your local mosque or religious authority.
